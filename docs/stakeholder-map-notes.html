WhereToI ecosystem map

Who helps a person decide which toilet is clean, usable, open, and worth walking to?

Map logic

The closer a stakeholder is to the centre, the more directly they shape the urgent toilet-access decision.

  • Core: the WhereToI decision moment.
  • Direct users: people searching, filtering, deciding, and contributing feedback.
  • Trust layer: people and systems that keep data useful, fresh, safe, and interpretable.
  • Wider context: organisations that own facilities, set rules, or could support future pilots.
